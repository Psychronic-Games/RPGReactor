# RPG Reactor 0.99.1 - 2026-10-24

Work since 0.99.0. The short version is in the [root changelog](../../changelog/0.99.1.md). Runtime **20261023.2**.

### Changed

- **Battle climbing and falls:** `ReactorPhysics.tacticalEdge`/`tacticalEdges` offer a wall climb (no ladder) up to the highest wall climbed at the climb cost, playing the Climb motion, and a drop of any height. Past the safe fall it costs the fall rule's share of max HP per tile; the preview shows it in red and "Fatal fall" when lethal. Units step over an eave or verge to leave a roof. Settings in `reactorPhysics`: `battleClimb` (on), `climbHeight` (6), `climbCost` (0.75), `battleFall` `hurt` (default) / `safe` / `off` (no deeper than the safe fall). `reactorPhysics.lethalFall`: `ask` (default; a Stay/Jump question in the battle menu window, words in Terms › Battles on the Map: `fallAsk`, `fallYes`, `fallNo`, `fallLethal`) / `allow` / `never`. Only the player's own unit on its turn may take a lethal fall; the AI weighs fall damage and never takes one. Action/First Person: a unit's fall hurts that unit alone, Jump against a wall climbs it, melee AI climbs walls. Runtime events `fightGrapple`, `fightFall`. Test `battle-climb-fall-grapple`.
- **Grappler:** `rrGrapple` on an armor (`range` 6, `height` 10, `uses` 0 = no limit, `cooldown` 2, `cost` "move" with `moveCost` 2, or "action", which ends the turn on arrival), edited in a Grapple section on Database › Armors (`database/GrappleSection.js`). Grapple edges show teal in the move range; a line from the hand, the pull and a climb onto the edge. Actors only (enemies have no equipment). Demo armor 114 "Grappler", worn by Fleagus.
- **Buff Rate (trait 15)** by Xehanort88 ([#77](https://github.com/Psychronic-Games/RPGReactor/pull/77)): `{ code: 15, dataId: paramId, value }`, `buffRate` by `traitsPi`, one roll in `isBuffAccepted` (none at 0% or 100%, so the random sequence is unchanged without the trait). `Game_Battler.installBuffRateGate` wraps the outermost `addBuff` and `itemEffectAddBuff` once plugins have loaded, so a rejected buff stops before plugin TP or voice hooks. Installed on every boot path.
- **Border crossing:** the light-combine programs (all four water variants), haze and halo compile in the background while a neighbor stands; a material returning to view in the game waits for its compile (`compileAsync`) instead of compiling in the frame; the crossing frame keeps the last frame's picture (`Reactor3D.Neighbors.KEEP_PICTURE`) and skips the second sprite-set update; the map left behind and its piece index are reused, not re-read; neighbor maps keep separate rock-height caches; neighbor bookkeeping and new models are spread over frames; the glow-water light texture builds in idle time. Longest crossing frame on the RTX 67–167 ms → 17–33 ms, on an integrated Radeon 67–150 → 33–50 ms; the rest is the map scene rebuild. Test `border-crossing-frame`, harness `perf/nw-border-crossing.cjs`.
- **Editor 2D neighbors:** `utils/MapNeighbor2D.js` draws a neighbor's tiles past the edge, dimmed (`RRMapNeighbor2D.DIM`), in 16×16-tile chunks as they come into view, redrawn only when the neighbor's file, shift or link changes. The view pans half a game screen past that edge. View-only; the Surroundings checkbox hides it. Test `map-neighbor-2d`.
- **Round parts by size:** `RRConstructionPatterns.roundSides(r)` = π·√(r / 0.4 mm), even, 8–64, keeping a polygon edge within 0.2 mm of the circle. Equipment `Mesh` spheres and tubes and Prop Builder lathes, spheres and tubes use it instead of fixed 16×32 / 32×16 floors; a larger count the user asks for is kept, and `ExactMesh` creatures are unchanged. Spheres keep exact ellipsoid normals through later normal passes and merges. A 9 mm clasp 1,024 → 256 triangles; all 70 accessories 229,234 → 159,750; longsword 13,372 → 7,544. Test `equipment-round-tessellation`.
- **Demo:** Tree-01 (prop 6) removed from North Haven's square; each of Rhea's roof cells sees 4 of the 5 raiders.
- **Self switches A to Z:** the page condition, Control Self Switch (a 13×2 grid of letters) and Conditional Branch offer A to Z. The runtime already accepted any letter; A to D keep their meaning. Removing a battle unit clears every self switch under its map and event id, not only A to D. Test `self-switch-letters`.
- **Effekseer size:** `Sprite_Animation.effekseerProjection` draws one effect unit at 2048·h / (h + 40960) pixels, as MZ does (it was h ÷ 26, 0.79 of MZ), inside the square viewport centered on the target. Measured against a plain MZ 1.9 project with Fire One 1, Slash Physical, Heal One 1 and Heal All 1, in Classic battles and on 2D maps: 0.96 to 1.07 of MZ's size, apart from fast effects that screenshots can catch off their peak. The in-scene path (box tracking, models' own effects) is unchanged. Battle Room Action Sequence steps are sized once when they start, so one effect unit matches the Classic pixels at that moment's room camera. The projection is `ReactorBattleData.effekseerProjection` (mirrored in `editor/src/battle/BattleData.js`), which the Database › Animations preview, its effect picker and both animation pickers also call, so previews show the game's size. Test `effekseer-scale`.
