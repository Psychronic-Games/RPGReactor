Seen on the hidden display under NW.js: opened at 900 × 698 with pin, minimize and fill buttons and a chip in the editor's window row; a track played from it; sized to 480 × 520 and 1100 × 700 with the list taking the room; closed and reopened at the same place and size; filled 1920 × 1080 and came back; no errors. Not seen: the in-page floating form (web editor), and the owner's real Wayland desktop. Test: `audio-player-window.test.cjs`.
