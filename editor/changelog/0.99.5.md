# RPG Reactor 0.99.5 (in development)

- **Development cycle:** advances desktop/web version surfaces, package manifests, the legacy import scaffold and bundled starter metadata to 0.99.5. Runtime revision 20261101.41 is unchanged; the runtime header and bundled copies carry the new engine version.
- **0.99.4 announcement:** saves the concise Markdown and plain-text itch.io devlog, with six image references/upload markers. The owner reports uploads complete, and the public itch.io listing confirms the [0.99.4 post](https://psychronic.itch.io/rpg-reactor/devlog/1746509/rpg-reactor-0994-released). Release history, known issues and next-session scope are recorded in the handoff.
- **Documentation:** corrects the CI record to 10 fixed and 24 remaining character failures, removes a stale push note from the online design, and carries the live demo test-data warning into the working list.
- **Character Creation in playtests:** the worker lives in `reactor-builder/`, which only deploys wrote, so a playtest's creator could not start. `PlaytestManager.stageCharacterBuilder` now copies the Forge into the project with `stagePlaytestCharacterBuilder` when a playtest starts, skipping the copy while a fingerprint of the Forge's files matches. `build-worker.js` leaves that folder out of the staged project and `.gitignore` out of the Demo.
- **Creator as game windows (runtime 20261102.1):** `ReactorCharacterPanel`'s HTML page is replaced by `Scene_CharacterCreator`, a `Scene_MenuBase` with `Window_CreatorTabs`, `Window_CreatorControls`, `Window_CreatorOptions`, `Window_CreatorColor` and a framed stage. The recipe, worker and 3D view move to `ReactorCharacterStudio`, which outlives the scene so a trip to `Scene_Name` keeps the draft. New characters start in everyday clothes regardless of the anatomy rule, and Back warns once after an edit.
- **Sign-in as game windows:** `ReactorAccountPage` is replaced by `Scene_OnlineAccount` and `Window_OnlineForm`, with start, sign-in, create and welcome pages. Fields are drawn by the window and typed into one real input laid over the row. Creating an account confirms the password; signing in fills in the last name used; a remembered account gets Sign out even with no progress on the server.
- **When characters are made:** Database › Character Creation replaces the Open on New Game box with a When choice over the same `onNewGame` field. The event command gains a dialog with `once`, `required` and `switchId`; a command with none behaves as before.
- **Option pages:** `DatabaseFlowForm` and `.rr-flow-page` lay out Online and Character Creation in as many columns as fit, with short fields side by side, secondary help in tooltips and fields greyed while their switch is off. Reactor Features uses the same flow. Seven new strings translated in all 17 locales.
- **Demo:** `ReactorOnline.json` and `ReactorCharacters.json` are tracked, pointed at demo.rpgreactor.org with playtests connecting. `updateCopiedTemplateProject` removes both from a new project.
- **CI:** fixes the 24 character failures and the audit. Regressions: soft joints count for their parent in cloth colliders and for garment anchors, legs lift before the rig so a merfolk tail stays joined, one-bone plates take no soft tissue, a chest patch's weld keeps to its side, and saved soft-tissue tracks drop redundant keys (1613 KB to about 326 KB). Stale fixtures gain `scale`, `userData.rest` and track-length handling. `overrides` move nw-builder's `glob` and `minimatch` to patched versions.
- **Poses (runtime 20261102.1):** thumbs keep their hinge on a planted hand; floor, squat and exercise legs go through `limitLegs`, and crawling knees share a hinge plane. The kneeling stretches are left as approved, with a labelled allowance in `character-3d-poses`.
- **Built characters' rig (runtime 20261102.2):** `CharacterGameWorker` never loaded `ModelRigger` and `ModelAutoRig`, so `savedModel` returned no rig without complaint and packages held 37 clips and a T-pose. The worker now imports both, `copyWebForgeAssets` ships them, and `forgeFingerprint` counts them.
- **Models released by a map change:** `ReactorCharacters.install` had no file for `loadModel` to read again, so a remote model swept by `ModelUse` logged a failed load and drew nothing. `Reactor3D.loadModel` now reinstalls a built model from its package.
- **Waking:** `progressOf` answers for a `.wake` with no `.save` (`saved: false`), and `wake` then keeps the new game and transfers. Notes no longer wait for events; saves are allowed between a battle's moves (`canSaveNow`) and the first comes after `FIRST_SAVE_MS`.
- **Riding and battle state:** new `state` action; `who` is asked for all nearby players every 3 seconds and carries `ride` and `fight`. `Game_RemoteVehicle` draws the ride. `cleanRide` accepts only plain model names.
- **Voice and chat hint:** `#reactorOnlineHint` shows both keys and `ReactorOnlineVoice.hintText()`. Leaving the window closes a held microphone.
- **Search commands:** `.command-picker-search` uses the theme's accent edge and placeholder on the tab bar's fill.
- **Parties (runtime 20261102.8):** `reactor_online_party.js`, with `party` and `news` actions and `parties.json`. `news` runs every 3 seconds and carries the party, invitations and the map's battles. Chat gains a Party channel.
- **Shared Tactical battles:** `reactor_online_battle.js`. The host streams `walk`, `act` (with rolled results), `sync`, `unit`, `gone`, `team` and `end` through `bSay`/`bRead`; `Game_RemoteActor` stands for a guest's actor; a mirror `Game_Fight` plays them, and its orders go back as `plan`. `startRound` takes any team name, and `F.tactics` reads a remote actor's weapon.
- **Portraits:** `ReactorCharacterStudio.portrait()` renders a 288-pixel face at Done into the package's `face`; `keepFace` makes it a bitmap and `ImageManager.loadFace` serves `Character-…` names.
- **Shared Classic battles (runtime 20261102.15):** `reactor_online_classic.js`. `Game_RemoteAlly` and `Game_RemoteActor` stand for other players; the host sends `croster`, `ready`, `cact`, `csync` and `cend`, and mirrors answer with `orders`. A mirror's `BattleManager` plays reports turn by turn, with temporary `$dataActors` and `$dataEnemies` rows that go with the scene.
- **Party values and following:** `world.partySwitches` and `world.partyVariables` (Database › Online), carried by `party`/`set` and `news`; `state` now reports a player's place, for `/follow`.
- **Watching and hand-over:** a `watch` side with no units; on a host's loss `successor` picks the next host, `takeOver` turns its mirror into the battle, and `bOpen`'s `was` leads the others to it. The Demo's party values are switch 13 and variable 16.
- **Shared real-time battles (runtime 20261102.21):** `reactor_online_action.js`. A player's unit stands on their `Game_RemotePlayer` (`peerSlot`); the host streams `apos`, `ares`, `afire` and `amotion`, and guests report `ahit` and `astate`. The Engine relays `bf` messages. `F.isSide` and `A.bodies` take "everyone but" (`!ally`) with `_manySides`.
- **Vehicles in shared battles:** a joiner's `vehicle` snapshot becomes `unit.vehicleBattler`, in the troop so an action finds its subject; `plan.via` reaches `beginAction`. A guest's battle now ends at once during their own turn, and a battle already ending is never taken over. Non-Defeat All goals no longer end a shared battle while other sides stand and the host's has fallen.
- **Shared battle fixes (runtime 20261102.30):** copied enemies now take the host's ground level, which First Person guns needed to hit. In real-time battles the voice key gives way to Guard. Getting on or off a vehicle mid-battle reaches the host (`whereNow`). Enemies left from a host hand-over are cleared. A battle just left isn't offered again. A real-time watcher leaves with Escape. The combat bar's picture check reads the web build's file list.
- **Online gaps (runtime 20261102.31):** `/duel` challenges (`party` `challenge`/`answerChallenge`, `news.challenge`/`duel`, `startDuel` holds an empty battle for 60 seconds). `ReactorOnline.respawn` replaces Scene_Gameover when `respawn` is on (Database › Online › Get up after a defeat). `DataManager.saveGame` also calls `saveProgress`. A host's message no longer holds a shared real-time battle. DESIGN-ONLINE lists what each player keeps to themselves.
- **Online and creator speed (runtime 20261103.1):** `fetchSleepers` draws the `SLEEPERS_SHOWN` (8) nearest sleepers, one per tile; the server's list goes to 200 so the nearest can be picked. Map 5 had 150 (147 of them test accounts), and every game drew 64 full characters. Online autosaves compress with `CompressionStream`. The in-game creator downloads three.js, the worker's files and a saved character together; the worker starts the body data on `tabs` (and on `warm`, sent at New Game), loads motions and the eye texture alongside it, keeps its build cache between builds, and `CharacterClothes.preload` fetches garment pictures in parallel. `Window_CreatorOptions` tries the highlighted choice on (`tryOn`/`endTry`, Cancel restores it). Editor thumbnails of worn things use `_thumbWearer()` (species defaults, gender in quarters, skin, hair, eyes, features), so sliders don't invalidate them and the disk cache hits; the panel's scroll refresh runs once a frame.
- **Crowds of 3D characters (runtime 20261103.7):** measured with 150 sleepers of the Demo's 149k-triangle actor on map 1, desktop GPU. Before: 46.8M triangles a frame, the CPU never idle, 51 fps. After: 5–7M triangles, about 40% idle, 60 fps. Arriving on map 5 (50 sleepers) the worst frame went from 916 ms to 33 ms. The changes:
  - The reflection capture draws only the 6 characters nearest it (`REFLECTED_CHARACTERS`, `hideCrowdFromReflection`). It had drawn every character again, 21M triangles a frame.
  - `GeometryDetail` (pixel-error levels, skinned bodies included) runs on any tier once a scene's last draw passed `HEAVY_TRIANGLES` (2M), not only on weak GPUs (`wanted`, `noteDrawnTriangles`).
  - `animationDue` stops sampling a model out of the camera's view (`inAnimationView`). A sleeper's pose settles over 60 sampled frames, then stops.
  - A held body (`holdModelMatrices`: still, pose held, no effects or worn gear) stops the matrix walk at its root and skips its bone-palette compare. `beamHit` reuses its bounds.
  - `swapPoses` writes and recomputes only models whose nodes changed.
  - Reflection passes warm their own shader variants (`ShaderWarmup.passHide`, `PASS_COMPILES_PER_FRAME` 24). A new character had been compiled synchronously at its first reflected draw (`getProgramParameter`, 900 ms).
  - `ModelJoins` paces joins while a map is in view (`mapInView`).
  - Online, `SLEEPERS_SHOWN` is 64, nearest first and one per tile.
  - Tests: `reactor-3d-crowds.test.cjs`.
  - Not fixed: sleepers lie half under the floor (the knocked-out `DownedPose` path hid them entirely, so it was not used). The Demo's actor models are 56k–149k triangles each.
- **Shared instance materials (runtime 20261103.8):**
  - Copies of a placed map model with the same surface, look and variant draw with one set of materials (`cloneModelTemplate(template, { shareKey })`, `Reactor3D._sharedModelMaterials`).
  - A copy takes its own before a flash or a variant changes it (`ownModelMaterials`). It is rebuilt from the template's material, with its surface and look applied again.
  - Other callers (battle rooms, portraits, UI previews, worn gear) still clone a set each.
  - 20 creator-built characters, weak tier: 6.3–7.3 → 3.5 ms of RTX 5070 GPU a frame.
