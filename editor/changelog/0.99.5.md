# RPG Reactor 0.99.5 (in development)

- **Development cycle:** advances desktop/web version surfaces, package manifests, the legacy import scaffold and bundled starter metadata to 0.99.5. Runtime revision 20261101.41 is unchanged; the runtime header and bundled copies carry the new engine version.
- **0.99.4 announcement:** saves the concise Markdown and plain-text itch.io devlog, with six image references/upload markers. The owner reports uploads complete, and the public itch.io listing confirms the [0.99.4 post](https://psychronic.itch.io/rpg-reactor/devlog/1746509/rpg-reactor-0994-released). Release history, known issues and next-session scope are recorded in the handoff.
- **Documentation:** corrects the CI record to 10 fixed and 24 remaining character failures, removes a stale push note from the online design, and carries the live demo test-data warning into the working list.
- **Character Creation in playtests:** the worker lives in `reactor-builder/`, which only deploys wrote, so a playtest's creator could not start. `PlaytestManager.stageCharacterBuilder` now copies the Forge into the project with `stagePlaytestCharacterBuilder` when a playtest starts, skipping the copy while a fingerprint of the Forge's files matches. `build-worker.js` leaves that folder out of the staged project and `.gitignore` out of the Demo.
- **Creator as game windows (runtime 20261102.1):** `ReactorCharacterPanel`'s HTML page is replaced by `Scene_CharacterCreator`, a `Scene_MenuBase` with `Window_CreatorTabs`, `Window_CreatorControls`, `Window_CreatorOptions`, `Window_CreatorColor` and a framed stage. The recipe, worker and 3D view move to `ReactorCharacterStudio`, which outlives the scene so a trip to `Scene_Name` keeps the draft. New characters start in everyday clothes regardless of the anatomy rule, and Back warns once after an edit.
- **Sign-in as game windows:** `ReactorAccountPage` is replaced by `Scene_OnlineAccount` and `Window_OnlineForm`, with start, sign-in, create and welcome pages. Fields are drawn by the window and typed into one real input laid over the row. Creating an account confirms the password; signing in fills in the last name used; a remembered account gets Sign out even with no progress on the server.
- **When characters are made:** Database › Character Creation replaces the Open on New Game box with a When choice over the same `onNewGame` field. The event command gains a dialog with `once`, `required` and `switchId`; a command with none behaves as before.
- **Option pages:** `DatabaseFlowForm` and `.rr-flow-page` lay out Online and Character Creation in as many columns as fit, with short fields side by side, secondary help in tooltips and fields greyed while their switch is off. Reactor Features uses the same flow. Seven new strings translated in all 17 locales.
- **Demo:** `ReactorOnline.json` and `ReactorCharacters.json` are tracked, pointed at demo.rpgreactor.org with playtests connecting. `updateCopiedTemplateProject` removes both from a new project.
- **CI:** fixes the 24 character failures and the audit. Regressions: soft joints count for their parent in cloth colliders and for garment anchors, legs lift before the rig so a merfolk tail stays joined, one-bone plates take no soft tissue, a chest patch's weld keeps to its side, and saved soft-tissue tracks drop redundant keys (1613 KB to about 326 KB). Stale fixtures gain `scale`, `userData.rest` and track-length handling. `overrides` move nw-builder's `glob` and `minimatch` to patched versions.
- **Poses (runtime 20261102.1):** thumbs keep their hinge on a planted hand; floor, squat and exercise legs go through `limitLegs`, and crawling knees share a hinge plane. The kneeling stretches are left as approved, with a labelled allowance in `character-3d-poses`.
