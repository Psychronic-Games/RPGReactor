# RPG Reactor 0.99.5 (in development)

- **Development cycle:** advances desktop/web version surfaces, package manifests, the legacy import scaffold and bundled starter metadata to 0.99.5. Runtime revision 20261101.41 is unchanged; the runtime header and bundled copies carry the new engine version.
- **0.99.4 announcement:** saves the concise Markdown and plain-text itch.io devlog, with six image references/upload markers. The owner reports uploads complete, and the public itch.io listing confirms the [0.99.4 post](https://psychronic.itch.io/rpg-reactor/devlog/1746509/rpg-reactor-0994-released). Release history, known issues and next-session scope are recorded in the handoff.
- **Documentation:** corrects the CI record to 10 fixed and 24 remaining character failures, removes a stale push note from the online design, and carries the live demo test-data warning into the working list.
- **Character Creation in playtests:** the worker lives in `reactor-builder/`, which only deploys wrote, so a playtest's creator could not start. `PlaytestManager.stageCharacterBuilder` now copies the Forge into the project with `stagePlaytestCharacterBuilder` when a playtest starts, skipping the copy while a fingerprint of the Forge's files matches. `build-worker.js` leaves that folder out of the staged project and `.gitignore` out of the Demo.
- **Creator as game windows (runtime 20261102.1):** `ReactorCharacterPanel`'s HTML page is replaced by `Scene_CharacterCreator`, a `Scene_MenuBase` with `Window_CreatorTabs`, `Window_CreatorControls`, `Window_CreatorOptions`, `Window_CreatorColor` and a framed stage. The recipe, worker and 3D view move to `ReactorCharacterStudio`, which outlives the scene so a trip to `Scene_Name` keeps the draft. New characters start in everyday clothes regardless of the anatomy rule, and Back warns once after an edit.
- **Sign-in as game windows:** `ReactorAccountPage` is replaced by `Scene_OnlineAccount` and `Window_OnlineForm`, with start, sign-in, create and welcome pages. Fields are drawn by the window and typed into one real input laid over the row. Creating an account confirms the password; signing in fills in the last name used; a remembered account gets Sign out even with no progress on the server.
- **When characters are made:** Database › Character Creation replaces the Open on New Game box with a When choice over the same `onNewGame` field. The event command gains a dialog with `once`, `required` and `switchId`; a command with none behaves as before.
- **Option pages:** `DatabaseFlowForm` and `.rr-flow-page` lay out Online and Character Creation in as many columns as fit, with short fields side by side, secondary help in tooltips and fields greyed while their switch is off. Reactor Features uses the same flow. Seven new strings translated in all 17 locales.
- **Demo:** `ReactorOnline.json` and `ReactorCharacters.json` are tracked, pointed at demo.rpgreactor.org with playtests connecting. `updateCopiedTemplateProject` removes both from a new project.
- **CI:** fixes the 24 character failures and the audit. Regressions: soft joints count for their parent in cloth colliders and for garment anchors, legs lift before the rig so a merfolk tail stays joined, one-bone plates take no soft tissue, a chest patch's weld keeps to its side, and saved soft-tissue tracks drop redundant keys (1613 KB to about 326 KB). Stale fixtures gain `scale`, `userData.rest` and track-length handling. `overrides` move nw-builder's `glob` and `minimatch` to patched versions.
- **Poses (runtime 20261102.1):** thumbs keep their hinge on a planted hand; floor, squat and exercise legs go through `limitLegs`, and crawling knees share a hinge plane. The kneeling stretches are left as approved, with a labelled allowance in `character-3d-poses`.
- **Built characters' rig (runtime 20261102.2):** `CharacterGameWorker` never loaded `ModelRigger` and `ModelAutoRig`, so `savedModel` returned no rig without complaint and packages held 37 clips and a T-pose. The worker now imports both, `copyWebForgeAssets` ships them, and `forgeFingerprint` counts them.
- **Models released by a map change:** `ReactorCharacters.install` had no file for `loadModel` to read again, so a remote model swept by `ModelUse` logged a failed load and drew nothing. `Reactor3D.loadModel` now reinstalls a built model from its package.
- **Waking:** `progressOf` answers for a `.wake` with no `.save` (`saved: false`), and `wake` then keeps the new game and transfers. Notes no longer wait for events; saves are allowed between a battle's moves (`canSaveNow`) and the first comes after `FIRST_SAVE_MS`.
- **Riding and battle state:** new `state` action; `who` is asked for all nearby players every 3 seconds and carries `ride` and `fight`. `Game_RemoteVehicle` draws the ride. `cleanRide` accepts only plain model names.
- **Voice and chat hint:** `#reactorOnlineHint` shows both keys and `ReactorOnlineVoice.hintText()`. Leaving the window closes a held microphone.
- **Search commands:** `.command-picker-search` uses the theme's accent edge and placeholder on the tab bar's fill.
- **Parties (runtime 20261102.8):** `reactor_online_party.js`, with `party` and `news` actions and `parties.json`. `news` runs every 3 seconds and carries the party, invitations and the map's battles. Chat gains a Party channel.
- **Shared Tactical battles:** `reactor_online_battle.js`. The host streams `walk`, `act` (with rolled results), `sync`, `unit`, `gone`, `team` and `end` through `bSay`/`bRead`; `Game_RemoteActor` stands for a guest's actor; a mirror `Game_Fight` plays them, and its orders go back as `plan`. `startRound` takes any team name, and `F.tactics` reads a remote actor's weapon.
- **Portraits:** `ReactorCharacterStudio.portrait()` renders a 288-pixel face at Done into the package's `face`; `keepFace` makes it a bitmap and `ImageManager.loadFace` serves `Character-…` names.
- **Shared Classic battles (runtime 20261102.15):** `reactor_online_classic.js`. `Game_RemoteAlly` and `Game_RemoteActor` stand for other players; the host sends `croster`, `ready`, `cact`, `csync` and `cend`, and mirrors answer with `orders`. A mirror's `BattleManager` plays reports turn by turn, with temporary `$dataActors` and `$dataEnemies` rows that go with the scene.
- **Party values and following:** `world.partySwitches` and `world.partyVariables` (Database › Online), carried by `party`/`set` and `news`; `state` now reports a player's place, for `/follow`.
- **Watching and hand-over:** a `watch` side with no units; on a host's loss `successor` picks the next host, `takeOver` turns its mirror into the battle, and `bOpen`'s `was` leads the others to it. The Demo's party values are switch 13 and variable 16.
- **Shared real-time battles (runtime 20261102.21):** `reactor_online_action.js`. A player's unit stands on their `Game_RemotePlayer` (`peerSlot`); the host streams `apos`, `ares`, `afire` and `amotion`, and guests report `ahit` and `astate`. The Engine relays `bf` messages. `F.isSide` and `A.bodies` take "everyone but" (`!ally`) with `_manySides`.
- **Vehicles in shared battles:** a joiner's `vehicle` snapshot becomes `unit.vehicleBattler`, in the troop so an action finds its subject; `plan.via` reaches `beginAction`. A guest's battle now ends at once during their own turn, and a battle already ending is never taken over. Non-Defeat All goals no longer end a shared battle while other sides stand and the host's has fallen.
- **Shared battle fixes (runtime 20261102.30):** copied enemies now take the host's ground level, which First Person guns needed to hit. In real-time battles the voice key gives way to Guard. Getting on or off a vehicle mid-battle reaches the host (`whereNow`). Enemies left from a host hand-over are cleared. A battle just left isn't offered again. A real-time watcher leaves with Escape. The combat bar's picture check reads the web build's file list.
- **Online gaps (runtime 20261102.31):** `/duel` challenges (`party` `challenge`/`answerChallenge`, `news.challenge`/`duel`, `startDuel` holds an empty battle for 60 seconds). `ReactorOnline.respawn` replaces Scene_Gameover when `respawn` is on (Database › Online › Get up after a defeat). `DataManager.saveGame` also calls `saveProgress`. A host's message no longer holds a shared real-time battle. DESIGN-ONLINE lists what each player keeps to themselves.
- **Online and creator speed (runtime 20261103.1):** `fetchSleepers` draws the `SLEEPERS_SHOWN` (8) nearest sleepers, one per tile; the server's list goes to 200 so the nearest can be picked. Map 5 had 150 (147 of them test accounts), and every game drew 64 full characters. Online autosaves compress with `CompressionStream`. The in-game creator downloads three.js, the worker's files and a saved character together; the worker starts the body data on `tabs` (and on `warm`, sent at New Game), loads motions and the eye texture alongside it, keeps its build cache between builds, and `CharacterClothes.preload` fetches garment pictures in parallel. `Window_CreatorOptions` tries the highlighted choice on (`tryOn`/`endTry`, Cancel restores it). Editor thumbnails of worn things use `_thumbWearer()` (species defaults, gender in quarters, skin, hair, eyes, features), so sliders don't invalidate them and the disk cache hits; the panel's scroll refresh runs once a frame.
- **Crowds of 3D characters (runtime 20261103.7):** measured with 150 sleepers of the Demo's 149k-triangle actor on map 1, desktop GPU. Before: 46.8M triangles a frame, the CPU never idle, 51 fps. After: 5–7M triangles, about 40% idle, 60 fps. Arriving on map 5 (50 sleepers) the worst frame went from 916 ms to 33 ms. The changes:
  - The reflection capture draws only the 6 characters nearest it (`REFLECTED_CHARACTERS`, `hideCrowdFromReflection`). It had drawn every character again, 21M triangles a frame.
  - `GeometryDetail` (pixel-error levels, skinned bodies included) runs on any tier once a scene's last draw passed `HEAVY_TRIANGLES` (2M), not only on weak GPUs (`wanted`, `noteDrawnTriangles`).
  - `animationDue` stops sampling a model out of the camera's view (`inAnimationView`). A sleeper's pose settles over 60 sampled frames, then stops.
  - A held body (`holdModelMatrices`: still, pose held, no effects or worn gear) stops the matrix walk at its root and skips its bone-palette compare. `beamHit` reuses its bounds.
  - `swapPoses` writes and recomputes only models whose nodes changed.
  - Reflection passes warm their own shader variants (`ShaderWarmup.passHide`, `PASS_COMPILES_PER_FRAME` 24). A new character had been compiled synchronously at its first reflected draw (`getProgramParameter`, 900 ms).
  - `ModelJoins` paces joins while a map is in view (`mapInView`).
  - Online, `SLEEPERS_SHOWN` is 64, nearest first and one per tile.
  - Tests: `reactor-3d-crowds.test.cjs`.
  - Not fixed: sleepers lie half under the floor (the knocked-out `DownedPose` path hid them entirely, so it was not used). The Demo's actor models are 56k–149k triangles each.
- **Shared instance materials (runtime 20261103.8):**
  - Copies of a placed map model with the same surface, look and variant draw with one set of materials (`cloneModelTemplate(template, { shareKey })`, `Reactor3D._sharedModelMaterials`).
  - A copy takes its own before a flash or a variant changes it (`ownModelMaterials`). It is rebuilt from the template's material, with its surface and look applied again.
  - Other callers (battle rooms, portraits, UI previews, worn gear) still clone a set each.
  - 20 creator-built characters, weak tier: 6.3–7.3 → 3.5 ms of RTX 5070 GPU a frame.
  - [Measurements](../../docs/PERFORMANCE.md#crowds-of-characters-and-online-players-2026-11-03-runtimes-202611031-8).
- **Battle music on arrival (runtime 20261104.1):** arriving on the map of a battle that plays the battle music (an online wake, a load that reloads the map, a transfer within the battle's map) played the map's music over it. `Game_Map.autoplay` in `reactor_fight.js` now plays `$gameSystem.battleBgm()` while such a battle is here and keeps the map's music as the battle's `_savedBgm`, for its end; the map's BGS plays as usual. `reactor_vehicles.js`'s silent-vehicle replay gives way to it. Online, `saveProgress` records `_framesOnSave`, `_bgmOnSave` and `_bgsOnSave` as `onBeforeSave` does (without counting a save), and `wake` calls `onAfterLoad`. Tests: `fight-music-on-arrival.test.cjs`.
- **Mirrors and water in a crowd (runtime 20261104.1):** measured in North Haven's raid at the owner's spot online (map 5, 50 sleepers, Tactical, RTX 5070 at 1280×720). The planar mirror pass (`Mirrors.renderLevel`, the square's water) drew 8.67M triangles and 5.7 ms of GPU, more than the frame's own draw (2.2M, 4.3 ms): it called `renderer.render` directly, so it took neither the distance levels `_renderScene` swaps in nor the crowd limit, as `prepareReflections` passed it no hidden list.
  - `renderLevel` swaps in `GeometryDetail` levels chosen from the reflected camera at the picture's height, before `narrow` crops the view, and sets `_active` while it draws.
  - `MapScene.mirrorHidden` keeps the `MIRRORED_CHARACTERS` (12) nearest the player in a mirror's picture; `hideCrowdFromReflection` takes the count.
  - After: the mirror 0.78M triangles and 2.7 ms; the frame's GPU 11.0 → 7.95 ms; draws 1,193 → 602; 50.3 fps with a 33 ms p95 and 234 of 1,200 frames over 25 ms → 60 fps (the headless cap), 16.8 ms p95, one frame over 25 ms. The CPU now idles about 12% of the time.
  - Tests: `reactor-3d-crowds.test.cjs`; `frame-waste.test.cjs` expects the crowd argument.
- **Moving shadows keep up (runtime 20261104.2):** the full tier drew two of its three dynamic shadow rows a frame (`dynamicPerFrame` 2, `dynamicSlots` 3). Walking where three lights cast (North Haven's square: the sun and two lanterns), one light's shadow of the player was a frame or two old on 63 of 66 refreshes, a different light each time: a shadow flickering behind a running player that settled when they stopped. `SHADOW_QUALITY.full.dynamicPerFrame` is now 3; after, 27 of 27 walking refreshes redrew every row. Weak and CPU-limited profiles keep their pacing. Test: `shadow-maps.test.cjs`.
- **Room for players' characters is a setting (runtime 20261104.2):** Database › Online › Server gains Largest character (MB) and Room for characters (GB), `storage.characterMB` and `storage.totalGB` in `ReactorOnline.json`, 0 for no limit, the default. `online-deploy.cjs` passes `storage` to the server's settings and `settingsOf` defaults it; `api.php`'s upload loses its fixed `maxModelBytes` (32 MB) and `maxStorageBytes` (2 GB) fallbacks, and only adds up the stored characters when a room is set. The `.gz` copy is written a megabyte at a time (`gzopen`/`gzwrite`), so no character has to fit in PHP's memory. Four strings in all 17 locales. Test: `online.test.cjs`.
- **Distant bodies skip the in-between pose (runtime 20261104.3):** `Graphics.FramePacing.capturePoses` leaves out a model whose holder has `blendPose` false (set by `Reactor3D.animationDue`: out of view, or under `ANIMATION_SMALL`, a twentieth of the view's height) or `matricesHeld` (a settled sleeper). Such a body shows its update's pose and place until the next update; its bones are neither blended nor walked twice a draw. Measured among 62 awake players at 1920×1080, frame cap off, one session alternating: 14.0–14.4 ms a frame with it, 15.3–16.8 without (a second session, with fewer far players, 0.4–0.8 ms). Test: `reactor-3d-crowds.test.cjs`.
- **Shadow stand-ins for built characters (runtime 20261104.4):** `Reactor3D.CasterMerge` (reactor_3d_lighting.js). In a moving-shadow row a part is drawn with its depth material (`casterMaterialFor`): one shared per side for every part not cut out, one each for cut-outs. A creator-built character is 17 to 39 parts against the Demo actor's 1, each a draw in every face and cascade. `Shadows._swapCasters(roots, true)` (the moving-caster pass only) merges a character's parts with one depth material and skeleton into a stand-in: positions through each part's bind matrix (attached skins land where their bones put them, whatever node holds them), skin indices and weights, and a cut-out's uvs, bound at the identity with the matrix given outright (so three never recalculates the skeleton's inverses). A stand-in lives in the scene's `caster-stand-ins` group on the moving-caster layer only, outside the model's tree, hidden but in that pass; the parts it draws for are hidden for the pass. Parts with morph targets join only while every influence is zero, else draw themselves; a part drawn at a distance level keeps its draw; a group with a part hidden or not casting sits the pass out; a face that culls a character hides its stand-ins (`follow`); stand-ins of characters gone from the scene are swept. Rebuilt when a part's material or geometry changes, not its visibility. Measured among the crowd on North Haven: one redraw of every row 1,842–2,173 draws → 1,455–1,498; all 255 mergeable parts of 14 characters were attached skins bound at the identity, so the stand-ins' vertices are the parts' own. `enabled` false: as before. Test: `caster-merge.test.cjs`.
- **Waking on the floor slept on (runtime 20261104.4):** a wake's transfer lands on a tile's ground floor (`Game_CharacterBase.locate` clears `_reactorGround`), so a player who slept on a roof woke in the room under it. `wakeNote` sends the floor stood on (`g`, tiles), the server's `wake` keeps it in the note and the sleeper, `wake()` keeps it in `ReactorOnline._wakeGround`, and a `Game_Player.performTransfer` wrapper puts it back after the transfer to that place only; `Game_RemotePlayer.setAsleep` lies a sleeper on it. Wake and sleeper notes are written aside and renamed (`noteWake`), so the sleepers list never reads one half written. Test: `online-wake-floor.test.cjs`.
- **Model pictures over the cap are resized before upload (runtime 20261104.5):** `capImage` left a GLB's oversized `<img>` for `ShaderWarmup`'s decode to resample, but three uploaded it at full size the frame it was first drawn, before any decode asked for it (`asked` false): every tier's 2048 sheets reached the GPU whole, the weak and low-memory caps included. `Reactor3D.fitModelTexture` now resizes with `createImageBitmap` before the texture sees the picture, holding a one-pixel stand-in meanwhile, and makes the storage again at the new size; without `createImageBitmap`, `capImage`'s canvas as before. Test: `runtime-gpu-load.test.cjs`.
- **Other players' character pictures at 1024, 512 on a weak GPU (runtime 20261104.5):** `buildGlbTemplate(…, { textureCap })` (a wrapper holds it in `_buildTextureCap` for the build) gives each texture `userData.rrTextureCap`, honoured by `fitModelTexture`, `capImage` and `ShaderWarmup`'s decode; `ReactorCharacters.install(id, buffer, own)` uses `Reactor3D.remoteTextureCap()` for anyone but this player (`remoteTextureSize`). Among 62 awake players (40 built) and 37 sleepers on North Haven at 1920×1080, RTX 5070: remote textures on the GPU 3.4–4.0 GB → 1.5 GB, those over 1024 127–154 → 1; the game's video memory about 9.1 → 7.5 GB; the raid with a moving mouse 14–20 → 27–28 fps (a fast-varying scene: 16 in one run), GPU 30–47 → 18 ms a frame, main view 31–46 → 12 ms, the worst frame 536–880 → 123–158 ms.
- **Unseeable parts of distant bodies left out (runtime 20261104.5):** `Reactor3D.FarParts`: a body under `ANIMATION_SMALL` (`blendPose` false) takes the parts the Forge names teeth, tongue, mouth inside, seams, stitching, zips, fasteners, indicators, lashes, brows and eyelids off the view's layer (layer 0) only, so they still cast and nothing else that shows or hides them is touched. About half a built character's parts. Its frame-time effect was within this crowd's run-to-run variation.
- **Crowd passes (runtime 20261104.6):** a mirror keeps the 6 characters nearest the player (`MIRRORED_CHARACTERS`, 3 on a weak GPU, `MIRRORED_CHARACTERS_WEAK`); in the square's water the characters were about 4 of its 6 ms. `MIRROR_WATER_SCALE` (1) can draw water's picture smaller; at 0.5 it saved nothing measurable, so it stays whole. `Reactor3D.ClosedMouth` takes a body's teeth, tongue and mouth lining off the view's layer while every face shape is at rest (lossless at any size). `Reactor3D.FarBody` (a remote player under a twentieth of the view as one merged, vertex-coloured draw) is built and off: in the raid's camera (15°) every player stood 128 to 279 pixels tall, walking the square most 180 to 530, where it would show. A `syncCharacterModels` wrapper placed above that function's definition was overwritten by it; it now follows it. Benchmark: `~/.cache/rr-tests/bench.cjs` (the raid, everyone frozen, cases compared in one session; three identical cases agree within a millisecond).
- **WebGL that comes back in a moment (runtime 20261104.7):** the NVIDIA driver (open module 615.78.08) refused GPU channels now and then (`NVRM … GspRmAlloc failed … NV_ERR_STATE_IN_USE`), the GPU process failed to start, and a playtest stopped at "Your browser does not support WebGL" (UltraMode7 switching itself off first) while a moment later it would have had it. `Main.waitForWebGL` asks before any script or plugin loads, every half second for ten seconds, giving each test context back; then reloads the page once (sessionStorage `rrWebGLReloaded`); then goes on to the usual message. Test: `webgl-wait.test.cjs`.
- **Draws by shader program; transparent parts in one pass (runtime 20261104.8):** counted per frame in North Haven's square (64 sleepers, 38 of them built characters; online and with the network loop stopped alike, 21.6 / 21.0 ms, so not the network; no objects, materials, geometries or textures made per frame): about 1,250 draws switched program on 890 of them. `Reactor3D.IntegerSort.opaque` sorted by material id as three does, and a built character's 27 materials, made together, use a dozen programs between them; it now sorts by the program three last drew the material with (`byProgram`, `programOf`), then material, then depth: 883 → 370–383 switches. three draws a transparent `DoubleSide` material twice and sets `needsUpdate` around each, working its program out again twice a draw every frame (54 a frame: soft hair, lips, brows); `buildGlbTemplate` now sets `forceSinglePass` on them: 1,161–1,165 → 1,085–1,086 draws, the game's own work a frame 7.5–7.7 → 6.6–6.7 ms, GPU 10.1–10.3 → 8.7–8.9 ms. Measured: the game's work as PIXI's tick (a `requestAnimationFrame` wrapper), since the headless browser presents at 60 Hz whatever its flags. Still to do: each material carries 64 uniforms, 60 of them the scene's (lights, shadows, water, cutaway), walked on every draw although GL keeps them per program (89 programs for 938 materials). Test: `draw-order-and-passes.test.cjs`.
- **A built character's parts merged at load (runtime 20261104.9):** `Reactor3D.LayeredParts.apply(template)`, from `ReactorCharacters.template` (not the creator's own preview) and `loadModel` for a library GLB from the Character Builder with a skin. Parts on one skeleton, opaque or cut out, become one `SkinnedMesh` per group, their vertices in the file's order (a pocket or seam lying exactly on its garment still wins that tie), bound at the identity; each vertex carries `rrLayer`, its part's row in a float table (5 texels: picture layer, relief/surface/cavity layers, normal scale, cavity strength, which maps ride `uv1`, side; shine; clear coat and alpha cut; glow and picture array). Pictures go into `DataArrayTexture`s: large ones by their exact size (copied as they are), small ones (512 or under) at the largest one's power of two, relief/surface/cavity maps in one linear array; one layer per file image (`userData.rrGlbImage`). The lit chain's last step (`LayeredParts.inject`) reads the row in the fragment stage and points `map_fragment`, the relief (`faceGlsl`), surface and shine (`injectShine`, its guard and `rrShine` scoped to its own block), cavities, glow, alpha test and per-part side (discard) at it. Groups: shapes (the face: union of morph targets by name, zero elsewhere; speech and blink find them on the merged mesh), the Builder's metal (`SPLIT_SHINE`), the rest; the body, hair and facial hair (found by name by worn pieces), blended parts and pictured glows stay apart. Merged meshes draw after the parts that stay (`renderOrder` +1, metal +2). Only the picture arrays a group uses are declared: with all four declared, a merged program with shine drew cloth washed grey (the water sheen term came out non-zero), which declaring only the used ones ended. North Haven's square (47 built characters, RTX 5070, 1920×1080, headless uncapped): meshes per built character 26 → about 8 (13 counting vehicles), draws a frame 1,080 → 512, frame 13.4 → 5.2 ms; eight characters drawn alone with their parts apart and merged: five within 90 pixels of 100,000 to 170,000, the rest hairline edges on plate armour (12,000 of 225,000 on full medieval plate), a chest plate (700) and one hem where the body showed through the shorts before (7,900). Test: `layered-parts.test.cjs`. Harness: `~/.cache/rr-tests/layered-ab.cjs` (apart against merged), `layered-check.cjs`, `layered-why.cjs`.
