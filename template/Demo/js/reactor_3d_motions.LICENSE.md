# Motion data

`reactor_3d_motions.bin` (the poses and motions of the shared motion library, `reactor_3d_motions.js`) is converted from trials of the Carnegie Mellon University Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database by `editor/build-scripts/build-poses.cjs`: subjects 2, 9, 13, 14, 23, 35, 76, 77, 79, 82, 87, 88, 90, 111, 113, 124, 125, 127, 136, 140 and 143, from the original ASF/AMC files, some with authored layers over them.

Source: http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/ ("This dataset of motions is free for all uses."; FAQ: "The motion capture data may be copied, modified, or redistributed without permission."). The data used here was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.
