# Open items (2026-11-01)

Open during 0.99.4: Character Preview 98, Props 62, runtime 20261101.41. This is the working list for the release in progress; [STATUS.md](STATUS.md#open-work) holds the standing threads and [OWNER-QUESTIONS.md](OWNER-QUESTIONS.md) the questions only the owner can answer. Changes are recorded in the [development changelog](../editor/changelog/0.99.4.md); released export/loading fixes remain in [0.99.3](../editor/changelog/0.99.3.md).

1. **CI on main.** The 2026-11-01 push was the first whole-suite run since 2026-10-29: 34 of about 8,900 tests failed, and `npm audit` failed. Nine failures in editor and build checks were fixed the same night. The other 25 are in character tests, from the character work since 2026-10-29, and need judging against the accepted visual result, not just new numbers: motion clips and limits (`character-3d-poses`, `character-3d-model`, `character-3d-merfolk`, `character-centaur-20261025`, `character-3d-cloth`), garment fit (`character-3d-armor-feet`, `character-garment-lining-20261025`, `character-warrior-bird-quality`, `character-bodyart-20261024`), abdominal and chest relief (`character-abdominal-definition`, `character-surface-refinement`), and live slider updates, which crash reading `clone` (`character-alien-presets`, `character-anatomy-controls`, `character-fast-drag-gown-20261025`, `character-preview-transition`). The audit reports 5 moderate advisories in `nw-builder`'s dependencies (glob, minimatch, readdir-glob, archiver), new since the last run; nw-builder 4.17.13 is the latest, and `npm audit fix --force` would downgrade it. The owner requested regular 0.99.4 source publication with these issues recorded; no clean full-suite or native-binary validation is claimed.

2. **Human faces and body appearance.** Preview 98 follows the face, lower-bust shading and abdominal iterations with broader Cheeks control, independent Cheekbones projection and a corrected native/replacement side-cheek join. Overall attractiveness and final face/body visual acceptance remain open. The exact pointy under-chin shadow reproduction has not been closed. Focused checks and native views do not establish aesthetic acceptance. [Current candidate and evidence](HUMAN-FACE-HANDOFF.md#current-candidate--preview-98).

3. **Face expressions.** The last recorded review (2026-10-31, before the shared sculpt) found the smile crease curling sharply past the mouth corners, open-mouth teeth with a visible center gap and gum above them, and crying tears rendering as bright jagged shards. The face has been replaced since. Later ledgers cover smiles but not crying or extreme expressions, so none of these is confirmed fixed or confirmed still present. Recheck them on the current face.

4. **Talking animals.** The jaw, mouth interior and Show Text speech are covered by Node tests and software renders only. They have not been driven in the real Builder or a game, and blinking and non-quadruped body plans are not covered. [Details](archive/handoff/0.99.4.md#2026-10-31--talking-animals).

5. **Regenerated Demo models.** The 2026-11-01 Demo checkpoint preserves saved recipes and thumbnails, which does not show that its generated GLBs and sprite sheets were rebuilt. Regenerate them before judging them or cutting a release, since geometry, skin materials, embedded clips and multi-frame sheets have all changed.

6. **Chibi visual quality (later; not the top priority).** The owner rejected the current chibi output as visually poor. Frame-to-frame consistency is fixed in 0.99.3; the style itself needs a quality pass. Improve head/body/hair balance, outfit silhouettes and facial readability at the target sprite size. Review complete exported front/side/back walking sheets at native size and enlarged, across representative bodies and outfits, before featuring chibi output publicly.

7. **Physical refresh and lighting acceptance.** Runtime 20261028.14 fixes metallic lamp response, local probe selection, capped presentation and the F2 callback count. Native controlled-clock and GPU checks are documented in [performance notes](PERFORMANCE.md#display-cadence-0994-runtime-2026102814). A visible 180 Hz town walk, vehicle ride and sustained low-end check remain useful; controlled clocks do not establish physical display throughput.

8. **Rhea's lines in North Haven** (Map005 event 12): 12 of her 15 messages now use her face set. Three still have none: page 1 commands 40 ("Raiders in the square!"), 137 ("Clean work.") and 141 ("They got the better of you.").

Earlier coverage only reproduced a close stall with SwiftShader. No app fix yet. Evidence: `scratchpad/popout-audit-20261029/close-hang-gpu.json`; ordinary nested checks allow 1 s before hiding. Investigate the native hide/preview cleanup path separately from the fixed popup bounds and animation-dialog ownership.

Native camera views cover the inn, north/south town houses and west cottage; sampled gable walls remain at least 0.034 tiles below roof skins across all 16 gabled building groups. No geometry change claimed. The location is an [owner question](OWNER-QUESTIONS.md). Evidence: `scratchpad/roof-refinement-20261029/`; inspection harness `smoke/nw-roof-sweep.cjs`.

Neither the plugin nor the project data was changed, and whether this is a compatibility gap or a project fault is unknown. No full Rebellion playthrough is claimed. [Evidence](archive/evidence/multiframe-20261101.json).

Removing it means updating the worker module lists and the alkalor and Scol3D credits in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md#character-face-base-heads). Wait until the face is accepted, so the older warp stays available for comparison.

14. **Online worlds.** Lite mode's limits are [listed in the design](DESIGN-ONLINE.md#using-it-today). Not yet tested: two real PCs over the internet, Windows and macOS builds, a real shared host, and a real stall on the live server with the catch-up limit. Character Creation is built, and crowds of custom models are untested. Two visual defects are open: characters built at standard quality show white patches on clothing (jeans waist, coat pockets), in the creator and in the game, and sleeping players sink into the floor with only hands, feet and part of the outfit showing. Engine mode is built, but on the demo server no game reaches it until `proxy_wstunnel` is on ([details](DESIGN-ONLINE.md#engine-mode)), so WebSocket through Apache is untested. Voice is built and works between browsers on the live site, but [real microphones and different networks are untested](DESIGN-ONLINE.md#voice). The next step is player-built structures. [Player-built structures](DESIGN-ONLINE.md#player-built-structures) and [group generation](DESIGN-ONLINE.md#group-generation) are recorded in the design.
