RPG Reactor 0.98.4: Build the World, Not Just the Map

RPG Reactor is a free, open source editor and engine for making RPGs. If you've used RPG Maker MZ or MV, you already know how to use it: open your existing project and everything is where you expect it, your maps, your database, your events, your plugins. No conversion, no import wizard. And everything new that Reactor adds is stored in side files, so RPG Maker can still open the same project like nothing happened.

Last release let you rig and animate 3D models right in the editor, no Blender required. This release puts them in your world.

Your maps have height now. Put a catwalk over the street and walk under it. Raise an event into the air, drag it up and down with 3D arrows, or move it in play with new Rise and Descend route commands. Your flat 2D maps keep working exactly as before, this is all opt-in per map.

Decorating with 3D models finally feels like a map editor. A ghost preview follows your cursor, every placement is one Ctrl+Z from undone, and a card of sliders handles position, rotation, and scale. Collision hugs the actual shape of the model, so players stand next to the console instead of bumping an invisible wall a tile away, and a passage view shows exactly what blocks movement before you ever playtest.

Your world can act without stopping the player. An event can fire a whole animation sequence, a tank raising its cannon, firing, and recoiling, while the player walks away mid-scene. Scripts can wait in the background for a switch, a timer, or an animation to finish instead of freezing everyone in place. Screens on models play real videos, effects live inside the scene and hide behind walls, and the camera can follow in third person or drop to first person.

Writing dialogue got a real editor. Edit an entire conversation as one list of boxes, watch a live preview of the current box drawn with your game's own window skin and font as you type, and see exactly which characters won't fit before a player ever does. Text codes come with a right-click menu and a reference that knows your plugins.

Any 3D model becomes game-ready on import. Grab something from an AI generator or an asset store, and the editor offers to optimize it on the way in so your game stays lean, with no visible difference. Rig it, animate it, place it, all inside Reactor.

The database keeps catching up with requests: see everything that references a skill or item with one right click, the Conditional Branch dialog RPG Maker users know, smarter enemy AI conditions, and more, much of it straight from GitHub issues. Keep them coming.

Try it right now in your browser, no download, the demo opens straight into a 3D room you can edit. Or grab the desktop editor for Windows, Mac, or Linux. Free and open source, and your RPG Maker skills carry over on day one.
