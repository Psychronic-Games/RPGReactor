# RPG Reactor 0.98.8: Bring Your Old Games, Build With Mirrors

Your RPG Maker 2000, 2003, XP, VX and VX Ace games can now come with you. 0.98.8 imports them into Reactor, gives you your own battle HUD and menus, and fills the 3D world with jumping, swimming, ladders, chrome and real mirrors.

RPG Reactor is a free, open-source RPG editor and runtime for RPG Maker MV and MZ projects. Same data files, same plugins, no conversion. Everything here is optional: a 2D project stays a 2D project until you switch a map to 3D.

## IMPORT ANY RPG MAKER GAME

That old project on your hard drive can run again, and you can keep building it.

- File › Import Project turns a 2000, 2003, XP, VX or VX Ace game into a new Reactor project, and never touches the original.
- Maps, events, the database, tilesets, window skins, graphics, audio and fonts all come across, packed games included.
- Each engine keeps its own rules: 2003 battle math, XP's pacing, VX's equipment, Ace's parallaxes and text codes.
- Ruby becomes JavaScript: event scripts and damage formulas translate, and over 110 popular scripts come back as plugins with your settings.
- MIDI, old movies and old images convert on the way in, and an Import Report lists anything the game was missing.
- Quests from journal scripts become Reactor quests.

## YOUR OWN SCREENS AND BATTLE HUD

Make the game look like yours, not like a default.

- Items, Skills, Equipment, Shop and Name Input can each be a screen you design, starting from a ready layout.
- A battle HUD of your own: faces that shake when hit, HP, MP, TP and ATB meters, state icons, sliding command windows and a cursor over the target, 3D models included.
- Build your own battle command list, including a command that runs a common event (a scan, a bestiary, anything).
- Text Input fields for passwords, codes and names.
- The editor canvas shows your screens exactly as the game draws them.

## JUMP, SWIM AND CLIMB

Your 3D maps become places to move around in.

- Jump with Space, with gravity, jump height and fall damage you set (Earth, Mars, the Moon or your own).
- Swim and dive in deep water, climb out onto the bank, and see the pool floor fade with depth.
- Climb ladders, step off at the top, grab one from above, and have your followers climb after you.
- A fatal fall throws the party down as ragdolls, and landings make a sound.
- Rigged characters walk, run, breathe, jump, swim and climb on their own, and hands really hold the ladder rungs.

## BUILD YOUR WORLD

Design a building once and place it anywhere.

- Build a structure on its own plot, floor by floor, then stamp it onto as many maps as you like.
- Paint floor plans room by room and watch them rise in 3D, with flat roofs for towers.
- Place 3D models from the Build bar, with move, turn and size handles all at once.
- Right-click to Copy, Paste, Duplicate or Delete models and built pieces.
- Resize stairs and ladders after placing them; a ladder can be any size, like 1.5 tiles wide.
- Make an event any size: one event can cover a whole ramp or a whole doorway.
- Pour water, mercury, tar, lava or slime, in any color, with its own clarity, waves and glow.
- A true Sun that casts long shadows from wherever you hang it.

## CHROME, GOLD AND REAL MIRRORS

Walk up to a mirror and see yourself in it.

- Give any 3D model a chrome, gold, glossy or polished surface, and it reflects the world around it.
- Give built walls, floors and ramps a mirror finish with the same sliders.
- Mirrors show you sharp and in place, near or far, and two facing mirrors reflect each other.
- Lakes mirror the sky and shore through their ripples, and looking up from under water shows the sky.
- Battles show the same reflections and shadows as your maps.

## ALSO

- Quests get an on-screen tracker, and rewards that give gold, items, gear, EXP or run a common event.
- Skills and items can hit HP and MP at once.
- Set every key and gamepad button on a new Controls page.
- Escape messages can be turned off.
- The editor fits a 1366x768 screen, and saving writes only what changed.
