# RPG Reactor 0.99.2: Centaurs, Sci-Fi Looks, Roads and Sprite Sheets from 3D

0.99.2 is out! The 3D builders got a huge round of new looks, species and creatures, your maps get roads, and anything you build in 3D can now become a sprite sheet for your 2D game.

RPG Reactor is a free, open-source RPG editor for RPG Maker MV and MZ projects: same data, same plugins, and everything new is optional.

![Ready-to-wear looks](../media/releases/0.99.2/01-character-builder.jpg)

**Ready-to-wear looks.** Dress your hero in one click: a space marine with a sealed visor, an astronaut, starship crew, a neon Glow runner or a street samurai. Cyberware, wasteland gear, tattoos and clothing that really hangs and folds are all in there too.

![Centaurs and more](../media/releases/0.99.2/02-species.jpg)

**Centaurs and more.** Meet the new centaur, plus insectfolk with compound eyes, the two-headed Hydrata, catfolk and werewolves, all wearing the same outfits as everyone else.

![Eagles, bats and big cats](../media/releases/0.99.2/03-creatures.jpg)

**Eagles, bats and big cats.** The rebuilt bald eagle glides on spread wings, joined by rebuilt bats, lions and tigers.

![Rebuilt vehicles](../media/releases/0.99.2/04-vehicles.jpg)

**Rebuilt vehicles.** Motorcycles and trucks with real wheels, doors and headlights, and body styles you tune with a few sliders.

![Trees in bloom](../media/releases/0.99.2/05-nature.jpg)

**Trees in bloom.** Oaks, cherry blossoms and weeping willows that change with the seasons and sway in the wind.

![Sprite sheets from 3D](../media/releases/0.99.2/06-sprite-sheets.jpg)

**Sprite sheets from 3D.** Export a walking character with six frames per step, or a tree that sways in eight frames, ready for your 2D maps.

![Paint roads on 3D maps](../media/releases/0.99.2/07-terrain.jpg)

**Paint roads.** Drag the Path tool to lay dirt roads, cobblestone or paving that follow the hills through your village.

![Search everything](../media/releases/0.99.2/08-editor.jpg)

**Search everything.** Type a word in the new toolbar Search to find maps, events, switches and database entries, one click away.

**Also new:** Reactor Features (switch off any Reactor addition when a plugin does the job), items that can't be sold, enemy stat variance and random drops, sprites you can rotate, flip and mirror from events, HDR effects, and much more. The full list is in the release notes.

Download it free and tell us what you build!
