## Models, first-person view and speech

- Database model switches stop previous animations/effects immediately and
  reject late preview/model loads. Right-hand animation/effect lists support
  clipboard shortcuts and Edit, Copy, Paste, Duplicate and Delete menus,
  including right-clicking blank space and empty lists.
- Face-point authoring designates eyes, mouth and lips, optionally following
  a part or bone. First-person view uses the authored eye point at the model's
  actual scale and retains the player's body and followers.
- Speak 3D Dialogue combines optional spoken text and voice audio with mouth
  movement, stop/reset behavior and interpreter waits. Speech samples a cached
  audio amplitude envelope; it does not perform phoneme recognition.
- Prop animation sequences survive main-menu return in 2D and 3D. Explicit
  stops and completed one-shots stay stopped. Placement-level animation speed
  can be changed by event command without replacing the shared animation.

See [face points, speech and playback usage](../../3D-FACE-AND-SPEECH.md).

  native/carried lights outside the specialized tools.
