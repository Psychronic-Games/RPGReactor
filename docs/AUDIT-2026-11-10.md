# Audit of 2026-11-10: what was found and not yet fixed

Twenty-one read-only reviews covered the runtime, the online server, the editor, the Forge tools and the importers on 2026-11-10 (runtime 20261110.10). What was fixed that day is in the [development changelog](../editor/changelog/0.99.5.md) under "Audit of 2026-11-10". This page lists what was found and left: each needs more than a one-line change, a decision, or a look in the running game before it is touched. Remove an item when it is done.

The audit of 2026-11-11 fixed 14 whole items of this page and parts of five more; they are taken out below and listed in the development changelog under "Audit of 2026-11-11". What that audit found and left is in [AUDIT-2026-11-11.md](AUDIT-2026-11-11.md). "For the owner to decide" is unchanged.

Nothing here was run in the game by the reviewers: every item comes from reading the code. "Confirmed" means the path was traced end to end; "plausible" means something on the way was not checked, and says what. Line numbers are those of runtime 20261110.10 and have moved a little since.

## For the owner to decide

1. **Fixed, top-down and isometric cameras held on the player never come in from a wall or roof.** `keepOutOfWalls` (`reactor_3d.js`) tests `resolved.focus`, which the camera's resolved state never carries, so only third and first person clear walls. Passing it through is one line, but it changes how such maps look wherever a building stands between the camera and the player.
2. **A model's base colour picture is clamped at its edges; glTF's default is to repeat it** (`_loadGlbTexture`, `reactor_3d_models.js:3848`). Merged built characters already repeat. Following the format would change unmerged parts with tiled coordinates (and could show seams on atlased models).
3. **A held click walks to the first tile and stops** (`reactor_scenes.js:960`): the walk is re-aimed only when the pointer moved. Stock RPG Maker re-aims every frame, so holding the button near the screen edge keeps walking as the map scrolls.
4. **A quest with the "start" rule comes back the frame after it is reset** (`reactor_quests.js:590`, `appear = starting || true`), with its rewards free to be given again. The `|| true` may be there for quests added to the Database after a save was made.
5. **Parties, invitations and challenges are kept by display name** (`api.php`). A name is its account's while that account is online or registered under it, but a character named otherwise ("Aria" played by account "doug") can be taken by anyone while its owner is away, and with it the party's chat and places. Keying parties by account is a change of the server's data.
6. **Project part files of the 2D Character Generator run as code when the Forge opens** (`CharacterGenerator.js:239`, `new Function`), with no question asked; Project Tools asks first.

## Bugs, confirmed

**Game**
- Scrolling a long quest in the log blanks its text, and the mouse wheel does nothing there (`reactor_quests.js:1043`, an override of `scrollBy` with another signature).
- Library motions lose their arm aims in the game, and Sword Swing and Bow do not move: `libraryRestSkeleton` passes no finger joints, so `handFrames` finds no hands (`reactor_3d_motions.js:1330`). `hamstringStretch` throws for the same reason (`:1867`).
- The game keeps only the height of the hips' movement in a motion, so feet slide about 15 cm in a squat (`reactor_3d_models.js:6396`).
- Materials of the party and a ridden vehicle stay on the list of the map left at a border, and are disposed while still drawn when that map's world goes (`reactor_3d_models.js:13338`, `reactor_3d_surround.js:1623`). May add to the crossing hitch.
- The model cache key leaves out the texture for OBJ, FBX, STL and 3MF models: two events with one model and different textures draw alike (`reactor_3d_models.js:126`).
- A neighbor map's glowing water has its haze placed a whole map's offset away once that map is played (`reactor_3d_world.js:4675`).
- `roofCapFor` swaps the art of every flat A4 wall autotile for its roof, raised or not (`reactor_3d.js:5432`); the facade table marks the wrong cells for a wall column shorter than its region (`:6038`). Both from a reviewer's small build, not re-checked.
- Battle Room: a sequence whose called routines leave it with no or two Apply steps lands its damage with no motion or animation (`reactor_battle_presentation.js:924`); the phase camera frames no other side when there are more than two (`reactor_fight_camera.js:1333`).
- Event commands: Spawn Unit's "How many" and "Stays on the map", and a goal's "Shown as" text, are written by the dialog and read by nothing in the runtime.
- Database › System 1 › Enable Message Skip does nothing: the runtime has none of RPG Maker MZ 1.10's message-skip code.
- A character flash on a character its 3D model draws never shows, and lasts as many times longer as its sprite is throttled (`reactor_sprites.js:7492`).
- `ReactorBackend`: a relaunch may drop the game's folder when the game was started with another plain argument (`reactor_main.js:406`, plausible: depends on what `nw.App.fullArgv` holds).

**Online**
- Waking loads the last full save (up to a minute old) and then stands the player where the newest note says (five seconds old), on another map if need be: past a one-way event with the switches from before it (`reactor_online_ui.js:139`).
- The Engine refuses a second game-made relocation within a second and the game obeys by moving the player back, mid-event (`engine.cjs:183`).
- Real-time battle messages go only down the Engine's relay when the sender has a socket, though the receiver may have none (`reactor_online_action.js:24`).
- `/follow` transfers the follower to whatever place the followed game reports; only the map's existence is checked (`reactor_online_party.js:124`).
- A stranger can still write up to 16 KB of join lines per request into a battle's log (capped today from 64 KB); the host's game silently drops a 413 (`api.php`, `reactor_online_battle.js:152`). No limit per address on `join`, or per player on `bOpen`.
- Every call to the Engine carries the server's master secret to whatever listens on its local port (`common.php:550`): on a shared host, another tenant could take it while the Engine is stopped. The Engine should have a key of its own.

**Editor**
- The project lock cannot see an editor on the other computer: through Dropbox, both open the same project and save whole files over each other (`ProjectController.js:157`). Record the host name in the lock and ask.
- A build stages the whole game in the system temp folder (RAM on the desktop); Cancel ends the worker without its clean-up, so the copy stays until reboot. An output folder inside the project is swallowed into the next build. The previous build is deleted before the new one exists. Old packs stay on a server after each packed deploy (`build-worker.js`, `BuildManager.js`, `DistEditorManager.js`).
- One pending "Unsaved Changes" question answers every caller: asked about the map, answered "Discard", and a close of the editor that came meanwhile discards the Database's edits too (`UIManager.js:931`).
- Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V on the map canvas copy and paste the whole map (`UIManager.js:719`); Delete was already limited to the map list.
- The Plugin Manager saves its own list over a manifest the Database's quest log has rewritten (VisuStella quest log projects; `PluginManager.js:128`). A parameter with no saved value is shown and never written; one whose `@parent` is unknown is never drawn; an empty list or struct value cannot be opened in the Structure view; a localized struct block replaces the English one (`PluginAnnotations.js:116`); a stored empty number becomes "0" (`PluginParamCodec.js:47`).
- Map Properties clears a battleback name that is not in the folder listing (`ProjectController.js:2272`).
- Database: 3D shape edits on the Tilesets page are written to disk at once, so Cancel cannot take them back; list Undo restores the whole section from an old snapshot and loses edits made since; a record pasted from another project keeps the old 3D model; Change Maximum on User Interfaces leaves System's assignment pointing at the removed record; the Troop and Common Event lists keep old names after an edit; "Referenced by" misses most uses of an Action Sequence; trait percentages with decimals are shown rounded and rewritten; the tile size radio changes the open map at once and Cancel does not put it back; a Quests switch cannot be set back to none; Battle Test's Cancel keeps the party edits; drawing some pages writes default keys, so Cancel asks about changes nobody made.

## Performance, measured or estimated

- **Shadows:** redrawing a static row or a sun cascade walks every static caster on the map (`reactor_3d_lighting.js:7063`). On the main thread, where a slow processor is short. The exact fix is kept in [AUDIT-2026-11-11.md](AUDIT-2026-11-11.md): it needs the Demo run.
- **Mirrors:** near a mirror or a lake on the full tier, the list of scored planes, a matrix per plane per level and some short lists are still made every frame (`reactor_3d_lighting.js`, `Mirrors.renderLevel`); the rest of the per-frame allocation went on 2026-11-11.
- **Effects:** an anchored effect looks its anchor up by name through the model's tree every frame (`reactor_3d_effects.js:264`).
- **Models:** every material that names a picture decodes its own copy of it (about 1.2 GB of 3.1 GB across the Demo's models, before caps); a collision mask is a fixed 513×513 grid per model and pose, never freed; every clip-driven bone is copied three times a frame; `BareBones` checks every body on the same update.
- **Textures:** each neighbor map uploads its own copy of the same piece pictures and, in 2D, its own 20 MB tile atlas.
- **Maps:** a failed click-to-move search explores 32 steps in every direction and is repeated at each tile (`reactor_objects_map.js:2277`); a hidden tilemap still paints every frame; colour filters rebuild their matrix four times a frame unchanged; materials' disk companions are asked for on every scene build.
- **Sound:** a sound effect not stored as `.ogg` walks the disk on every play, and every play reads and decodes the file again.
- **Tactical:** every cursor cell redraws every ground mark and reruns the openings pass.
- **Quests and media:** quest rules and media surfaces allocate per frame; screen texts of imported games run their codes and sort every frame.
- **Editor:** opening the Forge re-reads 279 style files (76 MB) every time; a Build rectangle drag copies the map's piece list for every cell on every pointer step; adding one model rebuilds every model on the map; a tile edit rebuilds the whole 3D scene; the Event Editor rebuilds its whole command list per change, and Copy on a 4,000-row page takes over a second; New Project copies the template on the window's thread; importing N files rescans the folder N times; the in-game creator downloads all 17 garment pictures before its first build; Export Model builds the character twice.
- **Leaks:** tile preview textures in the 2D editor are never destroyed; the 3D Models page adds window listeners on every visit; the Character Builder's view keeps every recoloured outfit picture (16 MB each) and its catalog pictures on disk are never pruned (9,328 files here); the Animation Generator keeps every video frame at full size; several Forge tools leave document listeners behind; projectile and first-person weapon models leave materials on the map scene's list.
