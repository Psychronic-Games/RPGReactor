# RPG Reactor 0.99.6 (in development)

Development is open after the [0.99.5 release](https://github.com/Psychronic-Games/RPGReactor/releases/tag/v0.99.5). These notes are for work in progress: most of it is checked by tests and not yet seen in a running game.

## New

- **Maps above and below:** in Beyond the Border, a map can have a map under it and one over it: a hatch to a cellar, the floors of a house, a room under a town. An opening marks where there is no ground between the two. You see the other map through it, with its sky from below, and you fall or climb through it with no fade while the party follows. A stair built in the map below comes up through it for a basement. The map editor outlines each opening and shows the other map there. The space between the two maps is counted from the lower map's ceiling, so 0 fits them together.
- **Splash screen:** Database → System 1 → Show Splash Screen shows `img/system/Splash.png` before the title.
- **Loops:** the Loop command can repeat a number of times, while a variable holds, or count a variable from one value to another.
- **Advanced Math:** Sine, Cosine, Angle, Divide and Square Root take a multiplier and a rounding choice, so their answers are useful in a variable.
- **Faces that move:** a face set named with its frames ("Name[4F]") talks in the message window, its mouth following the words, or sweeps into its expression; Show Text chooses Talk, Play once, Loop or Still. The Character Builder's face sheets can have up to eight frames a face, each face talking or sweeping.
- **Battle Camera option:** players can set how often a battle's camera cuts in (Off, Some, Every).
- **A troop's own battle mode** can be set on the Troops page.
- **Plugin options in custom Options screens:** an option a plugin adds no longer disappears when a project replaces the Options screen.
- **For plugin authors:** `Game_Enemy.rrActionPatterns()` and `rrEquipSource()` let a plugin supply an enemy's skills and equipment. Contributed by Xehanort88.

## Changed

- **Battle camera (Cinematic Cuts):** the camera swoops down to the acting battler and pulls back to the overview where it used to cut, and follows the action more gently. The hit is still a cut. Before the hit the shot is over the acting battler's shoulder, with its target down the line, and a hit that kills plays in slow motion for a moment.
- **3D Character Builder, Sprite view:** animations preview at the game's own speed, on a transparent ground, with clear names; zoom in and out; frame sizes include 144 (three tiles); the Smooth look has a soft outline, and the hard pixel outline is kept for the pixel art looks.
- **Character Groups, body and face ranges:** any body or face slider of the builder can be held to a range for a whole group, with Extreme proportions and Anatomical detail as in the builder.
- **Character Groups, a picture catalog and 3D models:** choose what a group can wear by clicking pictures: whole outfits, single clothing pieces, accessories and cyber limbs. A group can now also be created as 3D models, one per character, ready for 3D maps and battles.
- **Character Groups:** a group's walking sheets and battlers take the Sprite view's settings (frames, camera angle, look, outline, shadow with its angle and distance), and portraits can be one face each on shared sheets or a whole face set for every member, with talking or sweeping frames. The page scrolls as one, its scrollbars match the theme, how common each species is has a slider of its own, age, height, weight and muscle are two-handled sliders, and the page's wording is plainer.
- **Audio Player:** it is a window of its own now, like the Database and the Forge: drag it anywhere (another monitor too), size it from any edge, pin it on top, minimize it or fill the screen. It remembers its place, and stays open while you work on the map.

## Removed

- **The 2D Character Generator** is retired from the Forge. The 3D Character Builder exports 2D sprites in several styles and takes its place. Sprite sheets already made are ordinary pictures and keep working.

## Fixes

- **Battles in a Battle Room:** a character whose model came from Mixamo holds its weapon in its hands (the gun sat in the chest); a burst stops when its first round has downed the last target; motions keep time with the action on a slower machine; battles cost less each frame.
- **Editor:** a tint set to None no longer shows as a white box.
- **Building in 3D:** the see-through preview of a part now matches what is laid: a ladder several tiles wide shows as one ladder, a fence shows joined to the fences and walls beside it, a raised stair shows its supports, and parts on uneven ground each sit on their own tile.
- **Map Properties:** a 3D map's height is set there again, and the three sizes are labelled Width (X), Length (Y) and Height (Z).
- **Battles in a Battle Room no longer stutter** while a battler swings or fires a held weapon, or when an enemy collapses.
- **Quests:** a start-of-game quest taken back by Reset Quest stays reset; its rewards could be given twice.
- **Click to walk:** holding the mouse button keeps the player walking.
- **MV plugins:** lines and outlines drawn by older plugins appear again, in their own colour.
- **Online:** nobody can take an absent player's place in a party by taking their name; a guest still reading a finished battle reaches its ending.
- **Web builds:** a model whose download dropped is asked for again.
- **Editor:** "Save map as image" asks before saving and includes image layers; renaming a model's variant no longer breaks its uses; Cancel in the Database no longer asks about changes nobody made.

- **The test suite is whole again:** the seven tests that had failed in every run since early November pass. Nothing changes for players.
