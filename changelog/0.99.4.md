# RPG Reactor 0.99.4 (in development)

- **Voice as your avatar (runtime 20261101.40):** nearby players can speak to each other, and their characters' mouths move with the sound. **Database › Online › Voice** turns it on and sets the key, hearing range, who can use it and connection servers. Characters built in the game now carry mouth shapes, so they also talk in Show Text.
- **Fast connection (runtime 20261101.39):** where the host allows it, online games connect through a small Node process that PHP starts by itself, for pushed updates and movement checks. **Database › Online › Fast connection** sets the rate and the checks. Any host without it stays on polling. Adding to a shared variable now counts when two players do it at once, on either connection.
- **Sleeping, waking and a shared world (runtime 20261101.37):** players with accounts keep their whole game on the server. Signing off leaves them asleep where they stopped, visible to others, and they wake there on any device. **Shared with every player** in Database › Online lists switches and variables that change for everyone; events work as they do in single player, and an event marked `<world>` shares its self switches.
- **Accounts, chat and an owner page (runtime 20261101.35):** online games can use guests, accounts or both, and an account brings a player's character to any device. Chat has map and world channels, speech above heads, rate limits and blocked words, all set in Database › Online and enforced by the server. A new admin page lets a game's owner ban and remove players, reset passwords and take down messages. Online and character settings now live in their own files instead of System.json.
- **Character Creation (runtime 20261101.34):** players can build their own character in the game, in single-player and online games. **Database › Character Creation** chooses a list of actors or sliders, and what players may change; the **Character Creation** event command opens it at any time. It runs on the Forge's own modules, so Forge updates reach games at their next build. Online, a finished character is uploaded to the server and shared with everyone who meets that player.
- **Online settings and character choice (runtime 20261101.31):** **Database › Online** chooses Single player or Online and sets the address, player limit, update rate, party rule, names and which characters new players can pick. New Game opens a character and name screen. Running players now stay smooth for watchers instead of jumping after a slow server response; lag is no longer treated as a teleport. Title and intro maps stay offline.
- **Online worlds, first step (runtime 20261101.27):** players on the same map now see each other walk, with names above them, in 2D and 3D, on the web or a desktop build. **Deploy Game › Online Server** writes the game and a small PHP server into a web folder, with no database or router setup, and rebuilding updates it while keeping its data. Lite mode has no accounts or cheat protection yet. A game built without it stays offline. [Details](../docs/DESIGN-ONLINE.md).

Face/body visual acceptance remains open; the full test suite stays deferred. [Detailed checkpoint](../docs/HANDOFF.md#2026-11-01--character-and-rebellion-checkpoint).
- **2D event scroll alignment (runtime 20261101.26):** keeps stationary event graphics attached to their map tiles while walking in any direction. PIXI 8 tile layers now use the same rendered camera position as smoothly interpolated event sprites, removing the few-pixel drift visible around Rebellion's burn barrels. Game movement timing and authored event offsets remain unchanged.
- **Multi-frame sprites (runtime 20261101.25):** new and opened projects recognize tagged frame counts by default. Explicitly unchecking the setting persists through save/reopen and is respected by character/prop exports. Event-dialog pictures and map markers crop a single tagged frame; stepping previews use all frames. Multi-frame walk/carry sheets now sample the walking clip in every column instead of inserting Idle at frame 0; re-export older sheets for the smoother loop. Three-frame stepping is unchanged.

Work since 0.99.3.

### Added

- **Prop quality and new furnishings:** complete clothing interiors, cleaner fabric mapping and quilting, fitted cargo pockets, linked necklaces, closed goggles/hoods, improved harnesses and leather belts. Viking tunic colors and embroidery are editable. Tactical trousers, honeycomb/circuit shirts, a curved bar and six medical props join the catalog. Aircraft gain fitted cockpits and access details; bathroom fittings, executive chairs, stools and reclining medical beds receive construction refinements.

- **Wearable symbols:** independent built-in emblems or project pictures on capes, scarves and supported procedural torso clothing. Size, position, rotation, color and chest/front/back placement remain separate from fabric patterns, in both builders and saved models.

- **Wardrobe Wearables:** bundled clothing and armor components are also available in Props → Wearables. Character Builder's **Edit as wearable prop** carries garment choices and body proportions into Props. Saved garments retain skinning, body-fit controls and textured exports; Armor model equipment follows the wearer and hides covered Forge skin until removed.
- **Hair choices:** short/long center parts, combed back, balding, a face-covering side fringe, beehive and layered wavy bob. Combed cuts, bobs, long waves, bangs and ponytails have smoother flow and finer locks. Tall styles fit the Face view.

### Fixed

- **Wearable finishes:** sharp cape hem and scarf patterns replace blended vertex colors. Capes have a plain inner lining; scarves offer Solid, Stripes and Checks. Boot contours get finer sampling and additional smoothing along the outer edge.
- **Prop catalog:** consolidated wearable headwear, masks, eyewear, packs and harnesses; separated clothing subgroups; grouped interiors and stage gear consistently. Energy weapons sit with their families. The older duplicate Necktie is hidden while saved recipes remain loadable.

- **Boot and shoe soles:** smooth the shared sole/welt outline instead of carrying sparse foot samples into abrupt heel and side notches. Foot clearance, toe reach and heel length are retained. Applies to procedural footwear in both Character Builder and Props → Wearables.

- **Officer jacket:** removed the floating chest flap, competing zipper/button rows and bulky chest pockets. Shoulder colors join into the collar; fitted insignia, one concealed closure and shallow waist welts follow the finished fabric. Underlying uniform pockets stay hidden by the jacket. The same garment builds in Character Builder and Props → Wearables.

- Opening Forge directly on authored clothing no longer reads the body pack before it is loaded. Garment type changes preserve the reference body, and handoff retains the previous prop recipe in Undo.

- **Hendrix lighting compatibility audit:** preserve tile-mask alpha with source-in blending, including detached render roots; support GPU-only `BaseTexture.dispose()` during resizing; honor positional texture frames and legacy frame-update notifications; keep original billboard quads hidden while their child slices draw. All five project plugins were scanned. The lighting plugin’s optional old batch renderer retains its guarded filter fallback.

- **Procedural plugin textures:** restored `PIXI.Texture.fromBuffer` and `BaseTexture.fromBuffer`, fixing the missing-method error reported by Hendrix 3D Light and Shadow while creating fog noise. Byte/float RGBA data, sampling, repeat, updates and disposal retain native GPU textures. Buffer resolution changes display size without cropping the stored pixels. Verified with real GPU pixels and the Sang Hendrix lighting project across world, village, indoor and return transfers.

- **Combat ragdolls:** defeated characters collapse with loose limbs and momentum from the killing blow, using the existing fall physics. Bodies settle above the ground, stop simulating at rest, and restore normal animation and weapon handling on revival. The Physics ragdoll setting controls this effect.
- **Target hit chance:** after choosing Attack or a skill in Tactical battles, a readable hit percentage appears above a valid hovered target, using the same forecast as the target panel.

- **Grapple body motion:** the firing arm reaches toward the hook, the other hand joins as the rope tightens, and both arms support the pull into the ledge climb. Carried weapons are put away until the character finishes climbing.
- **Knocked-out characters:** fallen enemies and heroes use a resting pose with their weapon put away. Their posed bodies stay above the ground, and revival restores normal animation and weapon handling.

- **Game shadows:** Auto's CPU fallback keeps character shadows with a reduced drawing budget instead of disabling every shadow. This fixes North Haven showing shadows in the editor but losing them in a game with a saved CPU fallback. Newly attached weapons also use the correct shadow material, avoiding another WebGL feedback loop.
- **Tactical buildings:** interiors open only around the selected character after they enter a room. Outdoor characters, movement previews and roof targets keep buildings whole, making rooftop movement and grappling easier to select. Affordable grapple destinations also remain in the preview when they extend beyond ordinary walking distance.
- **Builder height:** the bottom bar explains “Placement height” above ground, with larger text and a solid background. Zero places pieces at ground height; Q lowers and E raises it.

- **Common Events commands:** Shop Processing and previously skipped commands open their settings before insertion. Reactor commands keep their own dialogs, and battle result branches survive editing. Choosing Fadein Screen starts with Fadein selected.
- **Database and event pop-outs:** menus and material/element pickers stay inside their own window when it is smaller than the main editor. Animation Cell Properties and the effect-file browser open in front of the Database.
- **Map-border WebGL warnings:** reflection captures no longer try to read their own render texture when water from nearby maps comes into view.
- **Faster Tactical battles:** character parts share their animation skeleton instead of repeating the same work for every body and clothing mesh. North Haven's raid renders substantially faster while retaining its appearance and smooth movement.
- **Metal armor in-game:** Builder metal uses a metallic light response, including colored lamp highlights, roughness, clearcoat and normal detail. Map ambient also provides a shaped reflection so dark armor retains readable form. Rhea can use a nearby reflection probe instead of reflecting from the camera.
- **Smoother high-refresh play:** render intermediate character poses and camera movement on every available refresh while preserving gameplay timing. Automatic quality no longer imposes a half-rate cap, and F2 counts actual new world pictures.

- **Compact builder toolbar:** the four group controls stay as icons at wide map sizes too, keeping the shared map/Blueprints bar short.

- **Motorcycle clarity and shadows:** Auto quality evaluates a 60 FPS stability floor while rendering stays uncapped. It no longer trades away resolution and shadows just because a faster display has unused refreshes; affected cached profiles are relearned. Parked bikes join interpolation when they start moving, acceleration zoom interpolates with camera motion, and transferred vehicles keep moving shadows.
- **Clothing that knows what's under it:** a new last step (`CharacterFit`) checks every garment against the real skin and the layers beneath it, both ways: cloth vertices against skin, and skin against cloth faces, so fingers, knuckles and other fine shapes can no longer come through the middle of a coarse face. Gloves, sleeves and collars hug the body on any proportions. Skin stays at openings, so you can't see through a neckline or armhole. Hanging skirts and linings keep their cloth-simulated shape.
- **Layers that don't thread through each other:** a shirt on a full figure no longer starts inside a waistband, which is what folded the hem back on itself in a ragged line.
- **Cloth shows what's under it:** thin tops rise over a raised nipple, sized from the skin, on any outfit; coats and armor stand clear.
- **Grey alien:** a true teardrop skull leaning back from the chin, with a large rounded brain, no temple points, a clean line into the neck, proper ear holes that match the skin, and more almond eyes.
- **Lips on every humanoid:** their own detailed layer with relief, reaching the mouth's corners: fine creases, a cupid's bow and a soft sheen, moving with speech and expressions.
- **Human faces (Preview 64):** a shared sculpt now blends continuously through feminine, masculine and intermediate faces while preserving colors, ancestry, sliders and expressions. Adds fitted blinking lids and exported fine skin detail; repairs mouth fitting and stale preview builds. Female defaults use long center-part hair. - **Face controls:** anime eyes no longer tear the face or flare the hair; Custom eye type stays selected; Blushing past its middle flushes any expression.
- **Slider camera:** dragging Gender or Height no longer snaps the view when released; the camera follows the body during the drag.
